How the ranking works

  1. The record comes first. An item on which the IMM sponsored, commented, took a position or filed is ranked high.
  2. Everything else gets a score from 0 to 100. The score says how closely an item's wording matches items the IMM engaged on, weighed against items it passed over. It was learned from 7,164 ERCOT revision requests and PUC control numbers since 2019, 97 of them with IMM engagement. Bills and notices leave no such record, so they borrow the same score and are ranked against each other.
  3. The score sets the rank. The cut-offs are placed so that, on items the model had not seen, 9 in 10 of those the IMM engaged on come out medium or high.
  4. Settled items stay low. Anything closed by October 31, 2026 without an IMM comment is ranked low. Anything still open is ranked by its score.
